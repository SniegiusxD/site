## Public proof

- **`/rezultatai`**: every published signal of the last 30 days whose match
  has started. For each:
  - its CLV against Pinnacle's close;
  - its result once graded.

  The page also shows beat-the-close rate and mean CLV by book (50 % marker,
  small-sample flag), mean CLV per Vilnius day, and a list filterable by book.
  Returns stay hidden until 100 signals are graded.
- **`signal_record`**: the site's own copy of started signals. The VM deletes
  `live_signal` rows 3 days after kickoff (`RETENTION_DAYS = 3`) but keeps
  results and closes, so without the copy the record could never show more
  than 3 days. It is filled on every results or landing read and by
  `/api/cron/settle`.
- **`/rezultatai/[id]`**: one finished signal with its own share card: price,
  fair price at publication (odds ÷ (1 + edge)), close, CLV and result. Signals
  that have not started return 404. These pages are `noindex`.
- Landing: a rolling 30-day CLV line under the fixed 09-01..13 study, and a
  header link. The tracker links to the record.
- Share cards (next/og) in the current brand: site-wide, live `/rezultatai`,
  per guide, per signal. The old `public/og.png` still said "Kraštas" and has
  been removed. Icons are recoloured and the Apple touch icon is linked.
- Numbers on 09-25: 78 % of 324 signals beat the close; mean CLV +2.4 %
  (+2.2 % without two outliers; median +2.8 %). By book: 7BET 81 %, TopSport
  80 %, Betsson 44 % (small sample).

## Accounts and support (launch gaps found and closed)

- **Password reset** did not exist. It is now:
  - "Pamiršai slaptažodį?" → `/slaptazodis` → an emailed one-hour,
    single-use link → `/slaptazodis/naujas`;
  - other sessions end on a reset;
  - the answer never reveals whether an address has an account;
  - the existing auth rate limits cover it.

  It sends only with `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM`, which need a verified
  domain (t135). Test addresses are never mailed. Checked end to end locally.
- **Change password** in the profile ("Keisti slaptažodį"), which signs out
  other devices.
- **`/kontaktai`**: a public contact form, since the help form needs a
  session. Messages land in the owner's feedback inbox (nullable `userId`,
  `contactEmail`). Limits: 5 per visitor per 10 minutes and 100 a day
  overall, plus a honeypot.
- **Owner reset link**: Savininkas → member → "Slaptažodžio nuoroda" (reason
  required, audited as `reset_link`). It creates the same one-hour link
  without email, for a member who wrote via `/kontaktai`.
- Found while building it: better-auth's `url` lacks `/api/auth` when its base
  URL comes from the request, so an emailed link would have returned 404.
  Links are now built from the token (`resetLinkFor`).
